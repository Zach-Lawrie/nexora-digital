About MEA

An integrated engineering business

MEA is more than an electrical contractor. We combine Mechanical, Electrical, Automation and Instrumentation under one roof.

Vision

To be the trusted engineering and training partner for industry across Australia and Africa.

Mission

Deliver safe, reliable mechanical, electrical and automation solutions, and build the skills of the people who run them.

Core values

Safety first

Every job starts and ends with people going home safe.

Practical quality

Work that is tested, documented and built to last.

Straight communication

Clear scope, honest timelines, no surprises.

Growing people

We train as we work, and share what we know.

Leadership

Lawrence Z Mumba, Founder. High voltage, instrumentation and control technician and industrial automation specialist, working across Australia and Zimbabwe.
