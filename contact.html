Questions people ask us

Do I really get to see my website before I pay?

Yes. We build a sample of your site first and send you the link. You only pay when you're happy with how it looks.

Which website package should I choose?

Starter ($80) is one page with your details and WhatsApp buttons. Business ($180) gives you up to 5 pages and a gallery. Pro ($300) is for bigger businesses that need a catalogue, booking page or news. We'll help you choose.

Why do I need hosting?

Hosting is what keeps your website online. Plans start from $7 a month, and higher plans include business email and updates.

How long does it take?

Most sample sites are ready within a few days of getting your details and photos. Starter sites can be live in about 5 days.

I don't have a logo or good photos. Can you still help?

Yes. We can work with photos from your phone, and help you put together a simple logo if you don't have one.

I already have a website. Can you take it over?

Yes. Moving an existing website starts from $40. Send us your website link and we'll tell you what we need, usually your domain and hosting logins. We make sure your email keeps working.

What if I stop paying for hosting?

Your site goes offline, but we keep a copy and can bring it back when you're ready.
