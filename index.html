Questions people ask us

Do I really get to see my website before I pay?

Yes. We build a sample of your site first and send you the link. You only pay the $300 when you're happy with how it looks.

What does the $40 a month cover?

Your domain name, hosting, security, backups and updates. When you want to change prices, photos or opening hours, send us a WhatsApp and we'll do it.

How long does it take?

Most sample sites are ready within a few days of getting your details and photos. Once you approve and pay, your site can be live soon after.

I don't have a logo or good photos. Can you still help?

Yes. We can work with photos from your phone, and help you put together a simple logo if you don't have one.

Can I bring my existing domain?

Yes. If you already own a domain name, we can connect your new website to it.

What if I stop paying the monthly fee?

The monthly fee keeps your site online. If you stop, the site goes offline, but we can bring it back when you're ready.
